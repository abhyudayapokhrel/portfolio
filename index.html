Projects

Mikha: Your Legal Eye

Transcribes photographed Nepali legal documents and splits them into clauses using the document’s own numbering. Each clause is checked against the Labour Act 2017 and the National Civil Code 2017 through embedding-based retrieval. It streams plain-language explanations with risk flags and citations, and adds text-to-speech and free-form Q&A.

  • Flask
  • Gemma
  • RAG
  • edge-tts

Lucid: Multimodal Mental Health Screening

An end-to-end platform that transcribes a spoken reflection with Whisper, then fuses MentalBERT text embeddings with Wav2Vec2 audio embeddings to classify the condition, likely trigger and depression risk level. I built the FastAPI inference server, the Node/Express and Prisma API, and the React frontend.

  • React
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • Whisper
  • MentalBERT
  • Wav2Vec2

VanRakshya: Forest Conservation

A multimodal AI system that detects wildfires, illegal logging and poaching from drone and CCTV feeds. A dual-model audio classifier picks out chainsaws and gunshots, and an interactive threat map lets rangers be dispatched in one click.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • Streamlit
  • PyDeck
